\subsection{Distributional Consistency: Results}
\label{subsec:consistency-results}

\textbf{Omnibus.} PERMANOVA detects no round effect in either split
(Table~\ref{tab:omnibus}). With 10{,}000 permutations the $p$-value resolution is
$10^{-4}$; both splits yield $p\geq 0.99$ and $R^2\leq 0.134\%$, indicating
negligible variance explained by ``round''.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Omnibus PERMANOVA across $K{=}10$ rounds (Euclidean on $\ell_2$-normalized embeddings; 10k permutations).}
\label{tab:omnibus}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
Split & $N$ & Pseudo-$F$ & $R^2$ (\%) & Perms & $p$-value \\
\midrule
Highly Contaminated & 5{,}000 & 0.7429 & 0.1338 & 10{,}000 & $\geq 0.99$ \\
Randomly Sampled    & 4{,}996 & 0.6476 & 0.1168 & 10{,}000 & $\geq 0.99$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Pairwise.} For the 45 round pairs per split, no comparison is significant
after BH–FDR ($\alpha{=}0.05$). E-statistics are tiny ($10^{-4}$–$10^{-3}$) and centered
near zero (Table~\ref{tab:pairwise-summary}); maxima are $<10^{-3}$.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Summary of pairwise Energy tests (signed, U-centered E-statistic).}
\label{tab:pairwise-summary}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrrrr}
\toprule
Split & \#Sig (FDR) & Mean & Median & Std & Min & Max & Max $|E|$ \\
\midrule
Highly Contaminated & 0/45 & $-5.14{\times}10^{-4}$ & $-6.31{\times}10^{-4}$ & $3.16{\times}10^{-4}$ & $-8.96{\times}10^{-4}$ & $4.49{\times}10^{-4}$ & $8.96{\times}10^{-4}$ \\
Randomly Sampled    & 0/45 & $-6.58{\times}10^{-4}$ & $-7.10{\times}10^{-4}$ & $2.08{\times}10^{-4}$ & $-9.12{\times}10^{-4}$ & $7.27{\times}10^{-5}$ & $9.12{\times}10^{-4}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Visual evidence.} PCA projections (Figs.~\ref{fig:pca-high}, \ref{fig:pca-rand})
show full intermixing by round (PC1/PC2 ${<}4\%$ explained each). Drift heatmaps of
signed E-statistics (Figs.~\ref{fig:heat-high}, \ref{fig:heat-rand}) concentrate near zero
without structure. Centered mean-distance maps and relative deviations
(Figs.~\ref{fig:centered-high}, \ref{fig:centered-rand}) show max absolute effects
$\leq 1.5{\times}10^{-3}$, i.e., ${<}0.25\%$ of the within-round scale.
